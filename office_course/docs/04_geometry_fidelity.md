# Lesson 04 — Upgrade office geometry

Edit the dimensions and furniture in `office_course/config/office_example.json`; regenerate the world and compare its floor plan with measured reality.

Represent furniture initially as conservative collision boxes. Later, detailed CAD/Blender/photogrammetry meshes can improve visuals without making physics unnecessarily expensive.

**Pass condition:** three independently measured office distances agree with the Gazebo model within your documented tolerances.
